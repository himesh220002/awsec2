Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026 · igvictory.com

1. Introduction

Welcome to IGVictory (igvictory.com). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is used, and the steps we take to protect your personal data when you visit our website, read our gaming transmissions, browse media archives, or interact with our live stream relays.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide you with a fast, secure, and personalized browsing experience:

  • Log Files: Standard browser and server logs including internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click counts. These are not linked to personally identifiable information.
  • Local Preferences: We use browser storage (localStorage) locally on your device to remember user settings such as 18+ age verification for age-gated game trailers and media volume preferences.
  • Voluntary Correspondence: If you contact us via email (support@igvictory.com) or our contact form, we retain your message and contact details to assist you.

3. Third-Party Relays & Embeds

IGVictory provides integrated media and broadcast relays for video games and streamers. These include:

  • Twitch (Amazon): Live streams, clips, and video-on-demand (VOD) embeds are powered by Twitch's official embed player. Twitch may place cookies and collect telemetry according to Twitch's Privacy Notice.
  • YouTube (Google): Trailers and official reveals are streamed using privacy-enhanced YouTube embeds (`youtube-nocookie.com`).
  • IGDB (Twitch Gaming Database): Game covers, release dates, ratings, and storyline data are fetched via server-side relay proxies.

4. Google AdSense & Cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to IGVictory and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or aboutads.info.

5. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to access, rectify, or request deletion of any personal data we hold. To exercise these rights, please contact our privacy officer at privacy@igvictory.com.

6. Contact Information

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please do not hesitate to contact us:

Email: support@igvictory.com
Website: https://igvictory.com
